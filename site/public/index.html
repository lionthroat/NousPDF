Nous PDF

A free PDF reader for your computer. Rearrange, copy and delete pages, search for words, and copy text. No subscription, ever.

You're on a phone or tablet. Nous PDF is a program for a Windows, Mac or Linux computer. Open this page on your computer to download it: the address is nouspdf.lionthroat.com

Which computer are you using?

  1. Download the installer

    Download for Windows

    NousPDF-Setup.exe · for Windows 10 and 11

  2. If your browser double-checks, choose “Keep”

    New programs get a warning like this until lots of people have downloaded them. It's safe to keep.

    In Microsoft Edge: click the ··· button, then Keep. If it asks again, click Show more, then Keep anyway.

    In Chrome or Firefox the button just says Keep. No warning at all? Even better, go to step 3.

  3. Open it, then click “More info” and “Run anyway”

    Open the downloaded file (click it in your browser's downloads, or find it in your Downloads folder). A blue box will appear the first time:

    “Unknown publisher” only means Nous PDF isn't paying Microsoft for a certificate. You'll only see this once.

  4. Click through the installer

    Click Next, then Install, then Finish. Nous PDF opens, and you'll find it on your desktop and in the Start menu.

    No password or admin rights needed.

  5. Make PDFs open in Nous PDF

    Do this once, and from then on double-clicking any PDF opens it in Nous PDF:

    Right-click any PDF → Open with → Choose another app → Nous PDF → Always

    Windows doesn't let programs set this themselves, so it's the one thing you do by hand.

    This only means Nous PDF isn't paying Apple for a certificate.

  1. Download the package

Using Nous PDF

Your pages are in the panel on the left. Nothing changes in your file until you save.

Rearrange pages

Drag a page up or down in the left panel.

Delete or copy a page

Click a page on the left, then press Delete, or right-click it for more choices.

Combine PDFs

Drag another PDF from your folder onto the page panel. A line shows where its pages will go.

Find a word

Press Ctrl+F and type. Every match lights up.

Copy text

Drag across the words, then press Ctrl+C.

Save

Press Ctrl+S. Changed your mind? Ctrl+Z undoes.

Questions

Why does my computer warn me about it?

Microsoft and Apple charge yearly fees for the certificates that make those warnings go away. Nous PDF is free and made by one person, so it skips them. The warnings appear once; after that it opens like any other program.

Is it really free?

Yes, no trial, no subscription, no ads. It's open source: anyone can read exactly what it does on GitHub.

How do I get a newer version?

Download and install it the same way. It replaces the old one, and PDFs keep opening in Nous PDF.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Nous PDF → Uninstall. Mac: drag Nous PDF from Applications to the Trash. Linux: remove “nous-pdf” in Software Manager, or sudo apt remove nous-pdf.
